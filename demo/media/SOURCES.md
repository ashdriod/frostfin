# Demo artwork: sources and licenses

All artwork in this folder comes from the Blender Foundation's open movies
(Blender Studio, https://studio.blender.org). It is used under the Creative
Commons Attribution licenses listed below.

**Changes:** images were resized and recompressed (posters to 600 px wide,
backdrops to 1920 px wide, logos to at most 800 px wide). Nothing else was altered.
